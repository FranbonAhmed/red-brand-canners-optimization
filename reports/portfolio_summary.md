# Red Brand Canners Optimization — Portfolio Summary

## Executive decision summary

The analysis demonstrates how optimization changes managerial decisions that would be difficult to make reliably from product-level margins alone.

- **Base LP:** $676,069 optimal contribution; paste demand and both grade supplies are binding.
- **Extra Grade A:** buy all 80,000 lb at 25.5 cents/lb; break-even price approximately 27.10 cents/lb.
- **Advertising:** only paste demand creates incremental value; maximum campaign value approximately $6,041.88 for 5,000 additional cases.
- **Extra Grade B:** reject at 18 cents/lb because marginal value is approximately 17.37 cents/lb.
- **Fixed setup costs:** open Juice and Paste; net profit $542,000.
- **Contracting under uncertainty:** 3.65M lb is the best quantity on the reported 50,000-lb grid, with expected profit $125,716.47.

## Portfolio extension

The fixed-charge line-selection problem is reformulated as a MILP with binary setup variables, providing a scalable alternative to enumerating every line combination.
